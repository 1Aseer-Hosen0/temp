## Brand & Style
 
The design system establishes an ultra-premium, museum-grade digital craft portfolio experience. Tailored for world-class design leaders, digital ateliers, and high-end creative studios, the aesthetic communicates intellectual restraint, hyper-curated precision, and monumental visual confidence.
 
The design movement synthesizes Minimalist Structuralism and Dark-Mode Editorial Luxury. Interfaces reject excessive ornamentation, relying instead on razor-sharp architectural alignment, generous atmospheric voids, dynamic cyan and yellow accents, hairline micro-borders, and disciplined typographic scale. The atmosphere is quiet, deliberate, and immersive—mirroring the serene luxury of a dimly lit private gallery where individual works of craft command complete attention.
 
## Colors
 
The palette operates in a tightly calibrated chiaroscuro spectrum infused with vivid electric highlights
 
- Surface Void (`#050505`) & Surface Base (`#0A0A0A`) Pure non-reflective deep charcoal blacks acting as the canvas foundation.
- Surface Elevated (`#141414`) & Surface Card (`#1A1A1A`) Low-luminance charcoal tiers that establish tactile physical layering without washing out contrast.
- Primary Text (`#F5F5F7`) A warm, crisp alabaster white delivering uncompromising readability and high editorial prestige without harsh glare.
- Secondary Cyan Accent (`#5bdcf7`) A vibrant, luminous cyan tone used for metadata highlights, active states, and interactive focal points.
- Tertiary Yellow Accent (`#fff14a`) A striking yellow highlight reserved for subtle interaction glows, curation badges, and micro-delights.
- Neutral Muted Tones (`#A1A1AA`, `#737373`) Neutral zinc and slate tones used for structural dividing lines and auxiliary body copy.
 
## Typography
 
The typographic hierarchy pairs dramatic, sculptural high-contrast editorial serifs (`Bodoni Moda`) with razor-sharp modernist monospace detailing (`JetBrains Mono`) and an ultra-clean neutral grotesque (`Geist`).
 
- Display & Headlines Set in `Bodoni Moda` with tight letter spacing and condensed leading to create high-fashion architectural impact. On mobile viewports, hero displays step down to preserve balance and avoid erratic wrapping.
- Indices & Structural Meta Key project enumerations (`01`, `02`, `03`) and chronological indicators are rendered in `JetBrains Mono` with oversized, low-opacity styling to anchor editorial layouts.
- Body & Longform Set in `Geist` to provide effortless legibility against dark backdrops, maintaining a relaxed 1.6 line height for long case-study narratives.
- Labels & Micro-Tags Rendered in uppercase `JetBrains Mono` with tracked letter-spacing (+0.08em) for technical precision.
 
## Layout & Spacing
 
The layout is built upon an asymmetric 12-column fluid grid governed by generous architectural margins and distinct vertical pacing
 
- Desktop (1280px+) 12 columns with `2.5rem` gutters and `5rem` page margins. Sections utilize large vertical rhythm scales (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to establish an unhurried, museum-catalogue pacing.
- Tablet (768px - 1279px) 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` margins. Multicolumn project lists collapse into staggered 4-column cards.
- Mobile ( 768px) 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer canvas padding. Grids stack linearly into vertical modules with `space-lg` card gaps.
 
Structural alignments prioritize edge-to-edge full-bleed hero media framed by micro-bordered inner containers. Asymmetrical offsets (e.g., project titles occupying column 1–7 while technical credits anchor columns 9–12) reinforce the editorial sensibility.
 
## Elevation & Depth
 
Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal luminance and subtle micro-borders rather than conventional drop shadows
 
- Tonal Layering The primary background rests at `#050505` (surface-void). Elevated surfaces and interactive project cards use `#0A0A0A` and `#141414`. Floating navigation drawers and modals reside at `#1A1A1A`.
- Micro-Borders All distinct surface boundaries are defined by sharp 1px borders using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. These hairlines provide crisp boundary definition against obsidian surfaces without introducing heavy visual weight.
- Cyan & Yellow Backlight & Halos Interactive hover cards and key focal elements utilize ambient luminous glows `box-shadow 0 0 40px -10px rgba(91, 220, 247, 0.08)`. On hover, the border transition shifts to `rgba(91, 220, 247, 0.35)`.
- Frosted Glassmorphism Sticky headers and floating controls incorporate a glass finish `background rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter blur(16px)` and a bottom border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
 
## Shapes
 
The design system embraces a Rounded  Modern Architectural shape geometry (`roundedness 2`). 
 
- Default elements (cards, containers, inputs, media previews) employ clean `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii, balancing structural precision with a more tactile, approachable finish.
- Modal dialogs, floating action islands, and showcase overlays utilize `1rem` (16px).
- Metadata badges, status tags, and interactive action pills use full circular rounding (`rounded-full`  9999px) to establish geometric contrast against the dominant card rhythm.
 
## Components
 
### Buttons
- Primary Dark obsidian pill with crisp alabaster text (`#F5F5F7`), a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, and subtle cyan inner luminance. On hover, background transitions to `#1A1A1A` with a micro-scale shift (1.02x) and cyan border brighten (`#5bdcf7`).
- Secondary  Ghost Transparent background with 1px `border-subtle`, paired with `JetBrains Mono` label text. On hover, fills with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- Editorial Action Unbordered inline text link featuring an animated hairline underline that expands from 0% to 100% width on hover.
 
### Project Showcase Cards
- Structured rectangular cards with `0.5rem` radius, bordered by `1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Media container features a desaturated or tinted initial state, shifting to full luminous color and a slow 1.04x zoom upon card hover.
- Top card header displays a two-digit index numeral (`01`, `02`) in muted `JetBrains Mono` alongside the project category tag.
- Card title rendered in `headline-md` (`Bodoni Moda`) across the lower boundary.
 
### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Compact height (24px to 28px) with full pill border radius.
- Background set to `rgba(91, 220, 247, 0.06)` with a hairline border of `rgba(91, 220, 247, 0.2)`.
- Typography styled in uppercase `label-mono` (`0.75rem`) in secondary cyan (`#5bdcf7`).
 
### Input Fields
- Sleek, low-profile inputs with a background of `#0A0A0A` and a bottom-only 1px hairline border or subtle four-sided stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- Placeholder text in `#737373`.
- Focus state eliminates standard browser rings, activating a 1px border transition to `#5bdcf7` with a faint `0 0 12px rgba(91, 220, 247, 0.2)` aura.
 
### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Monolithic square checkboxes (6px radius) and circular radio buttons with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` over `#141414`.
- Selected state fills with cyan `#5bdcf7` with an obsidian inner mark (`#0A0A0A`).
 
### Editorial Index Lists
- Full-width row items divided by horizontal 1px hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Grid layout index numeral (col 1), title & client (col 2–7), service disciplines (col 8–10), year (col 11–12).
- Row hover triggers smooth horizontal indentation (+8px) for the project title and initiates a floating cursor-following image preview thumbnail.
